Garner Recreation Center

A local guide to a fun place to visit in Garner, North Carolina.

Why Visit?

The Garner Recreation Center is located at 215 W. Main St. in Garner, North Carolina. It is a great place for people who want to stay active and enjoy recreation activities.

The recreation center is a 40,000-square-foot facility with three basketball courts, an indoor walking track, multipurpose rooms, an outdoor walking trail, a multipurpose field, and an inclusive playground.

Things to Do

  • Play basketball
  • Walk or exercise on the indoor track
  • Use the outdoor walking trail
  • Join programs and classes
  • Spend time outside at the playground or field

Plan Your Visit

Before visiting, check the official website for current hours, open gym schedules, programs, and other information. Hours and schedules can change depending on activities and holidays.
